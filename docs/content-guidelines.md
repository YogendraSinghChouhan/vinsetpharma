# Content guidelines

## Required business facts

- Vinset Pharmaceuticals is a pharmaceutical marketing company. It does not manufacture.
- Products are made by manufacturing partners and sold under the Vinset name.
- Packs state “Marketed by Vinset Pharmaceuticals”.
- Vinset shares product information with doctors and other healthcare professionals.
- The range is tablets, capsules, syrups and suspensions, injectables, nutraceuticals and personal care.
- The website lets visitors browse products and request a quote from any product page.
- There are no online sales or payments; the team replies by phone or email.
- The Mumbai contact point must be read from site configuration.
- The logo tagline is “Think about your life”.
- Intended audience: healthcare professionals and trade partners. This is an assumption and must be confirmed by the client.

## Allowed

- “marketed by”
- “our manufacturing partners”
- Intentions such as “we aim to”
- Statements about what the website does

## Forbidden

Do not state “we manufacture”, “our manufacturing”, or refer to a Vinset plant, factory, facility or laboratory. Do not claim certification, licence or approval, including GMP, WHO or ISO. Do not use numbers or statistics, years in business, customer counts, named clients or partners, testimonials, “best”, “leading”, “world-class”, “trusted by”, “pan-India”, “nationwide”, “worldwide” or guarantees. Do not promise delivery or coverage. Do not make quality-control, ingredient-sourcing, health or therapeutic claims. Do not mention incentives, gifts, samples or sponsorship, or describe how doctors are engaged beyond sharing product information with healthcare professionals. Do not give product indications or uses.

## Tone

Use plain, modest, factual, sentence-case copy. Describe Vinset as a marketing company, not a manufacturer. Keep product information general and direct readers to the label and registered medical practitioners where appropriate.

The audience statement and footer disclaimer are to be reviewed by the client’s legal adviser.
