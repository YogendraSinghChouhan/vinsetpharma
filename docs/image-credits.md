## Image Rules Compliance
- **No Identifiable People or Faces**: All imagery is purely macro, flat-lay, or abstract scientific detail.
- **No Visible Brand Names or Logos**: Only unbranded capsules, plain blister packs, and soft-focus uniform bottles.
- **No Facility / Factory Claims**: Alt texts strictly describe what is visible without claiming ownership ("Arrangement of sealed medication blister packages", not "Our facility").
- **File Budgets**: Every image is under 2000px wide and under 500 KB.
